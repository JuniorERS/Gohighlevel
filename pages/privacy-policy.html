Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 3, 2026

1. Who we are

Rip & Roll Turf (“we”, “us”, “our”) helps artificial turf installers get booked estimates with homeowners. This policy explains what information we collect through this website and our services, how we use it, and the choices you have.

2. Information we collect

Information you give us. When you book a call, contact us or become a client, we may collect your name, email address, phone number, business name, service area and anything else you choose to tell us.

Information from homeowners. When we run advertising for our clients, homeowners may give us their name, contact details, property details and project information so we can arrange an estimate with an installer.

Information collected automatically. Like most websites, we may collect basic technical information such as your browser type, device, pages visited and how you arrived at our site, using cookies and similar tools.

3. How we use your information

  • To respond to you and schedule calls and estimates
  • To provide, run and improve our services
  • To send you service messages, reminders and updates
  • To run and measure advertising for our clients
  • To meet legal, tax and accounting obligations

4. Text messages (SMS)

If you give us your phone number and agree to receive text messages, we may text you about your enquiry, appointments and our services. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP at any time to opt out, or HELP for help.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties. Information may be shared with service providers who help us deliver our messages, and only for that purpose.

5. How we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We only share it:

  • With service providers who help us run our business, such as our CRM, scheduling, email, messaging and hosting tools, and only so they can provide those services to us
  • With the turf installer a homeowner has asked to receive an estimate from
  • With advertising platforms, to run and measure ads
  • When required by law, or to protect our rights, our clients or the public

6. Cookies

We use cookies and similar tools to keep the site working, understand how it is used and measure our advertising. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, but some parts of the site may not work as intended.

7. How long we keep information

We keep personal information only for as long as we need it for the purposes above, or as long as the law requires, and then delete or anonymise it.

8. Keeping information safe

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Your choices and rights

You can ask us to access, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, or to stop contacting you. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under local privacy laws. To make a request, email us at hello@yourdomain.com. You can unsubscribe from emails using the link in any email, and from texts by replying STOP.

10. Children's privacy

Our website and services are not intended for children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the “Last updated” date at the top of this page.

12. Contact us

Questions about this policy or your information? Email us at hello@yourdomain.com.
